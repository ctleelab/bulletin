Manvika is an undergraduate student studying Artificial Intelligence at UC San Diego, 
with concentrations in Intelligent Systems and Computational Modeling. Her scientific interests 
center on building machine learning tools that help us understand biological structure and form. 
Currently, Manvika is developing an ML pipeline that converts 3D fluorescence microscopy images 
of cell nuclei into geometric meshes for nuclear shape classification. Outside of academics, 
she enjoys music and spending time outdoors.
